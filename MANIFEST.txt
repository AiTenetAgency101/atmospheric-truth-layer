  ❌ Average 6 temperatures = weak proof of agreement
  ❌ Easy to fake or corrupt
  ❌ No cryptographic lock
  ❌ Trust-based (believe us)

JARVIS:
  ✅ Uses Mandelbrot equation (z=z²+c) to prove agreement mathematically
  ✅ Impossible to fake (equation either converges or doesn't)
  ✅ Locked with SHA256 Merkle root hash
  ✅ Verification-based (verify yourself)
  ✅ Gaudí principle: simple rule → infinite emergence
  ✅ Anyone can audit the math
  ✅ Anyone can run it themselves

Key insight: z = z² + c IS the consensus mechanism.
